Problem Statement (Mobile App Development Contest)

Smart Campus QuickFix

The Goal

Develop a mobile application focused entirely on identifying, reporting, tracking and managing campus-related issues in a smart, transparent and user-friendly manner.

The application should help students and other campus users report common problems such as damaged infrastructure, broken lights, water leakage, cleanliness issues, network problems and other maintenance concerns.

Core Requirements (What the App Must Do)

1. Smart Issue Reporting & Location

Input: Allow users to easily select the issue category, provide a short description and specify the location of the reported issue.

Evidence: Allow users to optionally attach a photograph or other relevant evidence of the issue.

Submission: The application must provide a simple and functional mechanism to submit and record the reported issue.

2. Issue Tracking & Smart Prioritization

Tracking: Provide users with a clear mechanism to view their submitted issues and track their current status, such as Submitted, In Progress or Resolved.

Prioritization: The application should provide a suitable mechanism to categorize or prioritize reported issues based on factors such as issue type, severity or relevance.

Smart Features: Participants are encouraged to implement innovative features such as intelligent issue categorization, duplicate-issue detection, QR-based location identification or smart priority/severity prediction.

3. Campus Impact & Innovation

Dashboard: Provide a simple dashboard or summary showing relevant information such as reported issues, issue categories, status and/or resolution statistics.

Notifications: Where appropriate, provide users with notifications or updates regarding their reported issues.

Innovation: Participants may introduce futuristic features such as analytics, accessibility features, voice-based reporting, multilingual support, offline reporting, AI-assisted functionality or other relevant innovations.

Technical Mandate

The app may be built using a suitable mobile application development framework or technology of the participant's choice.

The solution must demonstrate a functional mobile application prototype with a clear purpose and working core functionality.

Participants may use suitable APIs, databases, mapping/location services or other technologies where required for their proposed solution.

Advanced technologies such as AI/ML are optional and are not mandatory for participation.
